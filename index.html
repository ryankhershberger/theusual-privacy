The Usual: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026
Who we are: The Usual is made by Ryan Hershberger ("we", "us"). Contact: hershapplications@gmail.com.

The Usual lets you save your usual orders at restaurants, share them, keep orders for family and friends, run group orders, follow friends and see popular orders. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

The short version

  • Your usuals are stored in your own iCloud account. We don't sell your data.
  • Things you make public (your profile and public usuals, votes and follows) are visible to other people using The Usual.
  • Group orders are the one exception to "iCloud only": the orders people send through a group-order link are kept on our group-order server for up to 14 days, then deleted.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads are personalized only if you allow tracking.
  • Your location is used only on your device to find nearby restaurants. We never store or share it.
  • Notifications are off until you turn them on.

Data stored in your iCloud account (private)

The app uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to store:

  • All your usuals, including private ones: restaurant name and address, the item, modifications, notes, rating, dietary labels, visibility, rank, any photo you add, whether it's "no longer on the menu", and how many times you've logged ordering it.
  • Usuals you keep for other people (for example family members), and allergies you record for yourself or for them. You only store a first name or nickname you choose. These are always private and never shared with other users.
  • Your settings, your list of blocked users, and your copies of group orders you started.

This data is kept in your private iCloud database. Apple stores it under its own privacy policy. We cannot see it. It syncs between devices signed into the same Apple ID.

Data that is public

When you use social features, the app writes to the app's shared public iCloud database, which other users of The Usual can read:

  • Your profile: username, display name, and the date you joined. The app also stores an anonymous iCloud identifier for your account (it does not reveal your Apple ID, name or email).
  • Public usuals: for each usual you mark public, the restaurant (name, address, map location), the item, modifications, rating, rank, dietary labels, whether it's no longer on the menu, and its photo if you added one. Notes, allergies, your order count, and usuals you keep for other people are never made public. You choose public or private for new usuals when you set up the app, and you can change any usual later. Making a usual private removes it, and its photo, from the public database.
  • Votes you cast on other people's usuals.
  • Follows: who you follow, with your username (so the person you follow can be notified, if they turned that on).
  • Reports you submit (the content or user reported, the reason and any details). Reports are not visible to other users.

Group orders

When you start a group order, the app sends our group-order server the order's name, the restaurant name and your display name, plus a secret code that proves you're the organizer. It creates a link you can send to anyone.

People who open the link (in a web browser or in the app) can enter their name, what they want, how they like it, allergies and notes. That information is stored on our server so the organizer's app can read it. Only someone with the organizer's secret code can see the replies. People who reply from a browser can edit their own reply from the same browser.

  • Where: our group-order server runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and D1), which processes this data on our behalf. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • How long: each group order and its replies are deleted automatically 14 days after the order was created, or sooner if the organizer deletes it.
  • Allergies entered in a group order are used only to show the organizer what to avoid. Please don't enter anything you don't want the organizer to see.
  • The organizer can save a reply as a private usual in their own app, which then lives in their iCloud account.

Sharing by text

When you share a usual or a group order, the app creates plain text (including allergies you recorded, so whoever orders can avoid them) and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You choose who receives it. We don't see or store what you share.

Photos and camera

You can add a photo to a usual from your photo library or by taking one with the camera. The app only receives the photo you pick; it can't see the rest of your library. Photos are stored with your usuals in your iCloud account. A photo on a public usual is visible to other users. Remove a photo any time by editing the usual, or make the usual private.

Location

If you allow "While Using the App" location access, the app uses your location to show nearby restaurants via Apple Maps. Your location is sent to Apple Maps for that search only. It is not stored by the app, not added to your usuals, and not shared with other users or with us. You can turn location off in iOS Settings and still search by name.

Notifications

All notifications are off until you turn them on in Settings:

  • New followers and Friends' new usuals are delivered by Apple through iCloud. The app asks iCloud to notify you when matching public records are created.
  • Group order replies are checked by your phone in the background and shown as notifications on your device.

You can turn any of these off in the app, or turn off notifications for The Usual in iOS Settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Usual is free and shows banner ads from Google AdMob on browsing screens (not on your own list of usuals).

  • Tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency): iOS asks whether The Usual may track you. If you allow it, Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads and measure them. If you don't, ads are not personalized using that identifier.
  • Consent (where required): In regions such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in some US states, the app shows Google's consent message. You can change your choices any time in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Google may collect device information, ad interactions, approximate location derived from your IP address, and diagnostic data. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Your usuals, notes, allergies, group orders and profile are never used for advertising.

How long data is kept

  • Private data stays in your iCloud account until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Public data stays until you make it private, delete it, or delete your account.
  • Group orders and their replies are deleted 14 days after they're created.
  • Reports may be kept for moderation records.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a usual by swiping on it or from its edit screen. If it was public, its public copy (and photo) is removed too.
  • Delete a group order from its screen (the link stops working and replies are deleted).
  • Delete your account in Settings → Delete Account. This removes your profile, public usuals, votes and follows from the public database, deletes your group orders from our server, turns off your notifications, and erases your private usuals, people, allergies, settings and blocked list from iCloud. Your username becomes available to others. Follows that other people made of you are hidden once your profile is gone.

Safety

You can report usuals and users and block users. We review reports and may remove content or accounts that break our rules (spam, harassment, offensive or inappropriate content).

Children

The Usual is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we don't knowingly collect their data. Parents can keep usuals for their children in their own account; those are private and stay in the parent's iCloud.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your data. Most of this you can do directly in the app (edit or delete usuals, delete group orders, delete your account). For anything else, contact us at hershapplications@gmail.com.

Changes

We'll update this page if the policy changes and change the effective date above.
