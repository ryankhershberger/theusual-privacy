The Usual: Help & Support

Need help? Email hershapplications@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a day.

Common questions

Why does The Usual need iCloud?

Your usuals are saved in your own iCloud account, so they sync across your devices and stay private. There's no separate password. If the app says it needs iCloud, open the Settings app, tap your name, and make sure iCloud is on.

How do I make a usual private or public?

Open the usual and choose Public or Private under "Who can see it". Private usuals are only visible to you. You can still share them by text.

How do group orders work?

Start one from a restaurant page or the + menu, then tap Send the Link. Anyone can open the link in a browser, with or without the app, and add what they want. Replies show up in your group order. Links stop working after 14 days.

How do I save usuals for my family?

When adding a usual, use For → Add Someone Else. Their allergies go in Profile → People & Allergies. Usuals you keep for other people are always private.

Why won't my photo save on a public usual?

Public photos have to show the food or drink. The app checks this on your phone (the photo isn't sent anywhere). Try a clearer photo of the order, or make the usual private to keep any photo you like.

How do I find my friends?

Open Profile → Find People and search for their username or name. To invite someone, tap Share Profile and send them your link.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap ••• on any public usual or on a profile, then choose Report or Block. We review reports within 24 hours. Manage blocked people in Profile → Settings → Blocked Users.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This removes your profile, public usuals, votes, follows and group orders, and erases your private data from iCloud.

Can I change my username?

Not yet. You can change your display name in Profile → Edit.
